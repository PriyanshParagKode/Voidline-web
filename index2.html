    COMBAT DOCTRINE
    01 · LEAD YOUR TARGET

    Aliens never stop chasing. Their swarm tactics punish straight lines — roll, cut throttle, and force them to overshoot.

    02 · MIND THE HEAT

    Pulse cannons build heat. If the bar hits full the cannons lock out until they cool. Fire in controlled bursts.

    03 · SHIELD REGENERATION

    Shields recharge after four seconds without damage. Hull does not. Break contact when your screen turns red.

    04 · MISSILE LOCK

    Hold RMB with a hostile inside the reticle. A full ring means a solid lock. Release to launch a homing salvo.

    05 · SALVAGE

    Destroyed hostiles may drop nanite cells and missile canisters. Fly through them to collect.

    TOUCH FLIGHT CONTROLS

    LEFT PAD steer · FIRE pulse cannons · MISSILE hold to lock, release to launch
